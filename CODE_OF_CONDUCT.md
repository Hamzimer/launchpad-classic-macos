# Community Code of Conduct

Launchpad Classic welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation must remain respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep issues and pull requests welcoming to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. For urgent abuse or threats, use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools. For project moderation concerns, contact the repository owner through the contact methods listed on their GitHub profile rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
